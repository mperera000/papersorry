# Phase 0 - Riskiest assumption test

**Belief:** close friends will open and enjoy this instead of a lazy "sorry lol" text.

**Pass:** of 10 real people, at least 5 say they'd smile, keep it, or send one themselves.

## What to send
1. Run the app (`npm run dev`) or deploy a preview.
2. Open the home page on your phone.
3. Send **10 close friends / roommates / partners** a message like:

> Quick favor: open this link on your phone, tap the letter, peel a few stickers.
> Would you keep something like this if a friend sent it after a tiny oops?
> Reply with: keep / meh / nah + one honest sentence.

## What to watch for
- Do they open it without you explaining?
- Do they smile / laugh / forward vibes?
- Does the paper + stickers feel special, or cheap?
